#### Educational materials for Material Flow Analysis

![Ressources](/files/inline-images/Panorama1_1.JPG)

Material Flow Analysis (MFA) is a systematic assessment of the flows and stocks of materials within a system defined in space and time. New developments and expansions in industrial research fields emphasize the importance of MFA methods to generate a meaningful understanding of specific system functions. It can also support decision makers, particularly in the expanding field of environmental policy.

An introductory course on Material Flow Analysis was prepared by the University of Bordeaux and can be used for educational purposes.
